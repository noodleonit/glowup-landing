Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LocalGrow.biz ("we," "us") runs this page to offer a free Glow-Up Report for local businesses. Here's what we collect and what we do with it, in plain English.

What we collect

When you request a report, we collect your business name, email address, city and (if you share it) your website. Like most websites, we may also collect basic visit data through cookies and advertising pixels, such as which page you came from and whether you submitted the form.

How we use it

  • To create and email your Glow-Up Report.
  • To send a few short follow-up emails with local marketing tips, which you can stop at any time.
  • To measure whether our ads and pages are working (for example, through Meta or Google advertising tools).

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information. Ever.

Who helps us

We use trusted service providers to run this page and send email (including our email platform and email delivery provider), and advertising platforms to measure ad performance. They only process your information to provide those services.

Your choices

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email kim@localgrow.biz to ask us to update or delete your information, and we'll take care of it.
